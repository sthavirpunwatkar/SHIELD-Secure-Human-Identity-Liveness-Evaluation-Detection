## Slide 1: Title & Problem Statement

### Slide Content
*   **Project Title:** SHIELD: Secure Human Identity & Liveness Evaluation Detection
*   **Sub-title:** Multimodal Real-Time Verification & Spoof Prevention
*   **Domain:** Computer Vision, Biometrics & Anti-Spoofing
*   **Problem Statement:**
    Identity fraud and spoofing attacks have compromised remote authentication services (such as automated attendance and virtual interviews):
    *   **Presentation Attacks (PA):** High-definition printed photos, screens playing recorded loops, and 3D silicone mask replays bypass standard facial biometrics.
    *   **Identity Swap Attacks:** Candidates swap mid-session (tag-team attacks) during exams or interviews.
    *   **Hardware Constraints:** Traditional solutions require specialized, expensive 3D depth cameras, limiting widespread deployment.

## Slide 2: Project Objectives

### Slide Content
*   **🎯 Core Objectives:**
    *   Develop an end-to-end, ultra-low latency (<100ms) multi-layered pipeline for biometric verification.
    *   Implement non-intrusive biological checks (physiological heart-rate validation via rPPG) without dedicated sensors.
    *   Construct interactive active challenge-response tasks preventing pre-recorded synthetic deepfakes.
    *   Establish an explainable fusion architecture to provide forensic audit capability for each verification decision.
*   **⚡ Engineering Target KPIs:**
    *   **ISO/IEC 30107-3 Standard Compliance:** Targeting ACER (Average Classification Error Rate) below 1.5% to beat enterprise systems.
    *   **Edge-Compatible Latency Profiles:** Ensure end-to-end processing (detection, crops, fusion, classification) runs in less than 100ms on CPU.
    *   **Zero-Dependency Webcam Deployment:** Operate smoothly over normal RGB standard 2D webcams, eliminating hardware friction.

## Slide 3: System Architecture & Methodology

### Slide Content
*   **Pipeline Flow:**
    `Camera Capture ➡️ YOLOv8 Face Detection ➡️ Signal Quality Gate ➡️ Multimodal Inference ➡️ Decision Fusion`
*   **Four Verification Pillars:**
    1.  **Passive Texture:** MiniFASNet CNN analyzing high-frequency texture cues to distinguish human skin from printed papers or screen pixels.
    2.  **Physiological rPPG:** 3D Spatio-Temporal CNN extracting blood volume pulse (BVP) from blood flow micro-fluctuations in skin.
    3.  **Active Challenges:** Randomized directives (Smile, Blink, Turn) generated by `ChallengeEngine` to ensure presence.
    4.  **Temporal Audit:** `TemporalValidator` matching challenge execution times and checking for jump-cut manipulations.

## Slide 5: Novelty & Custom Contributions

### Slide Content
1.  **Real-Time Scale-Invariant Identity Consistency Check:**
    Protects session integrity against mid-session user swaps (tag-team cheats):
    *   Computes scale-invariant 4D signature ratios (nose, eyes, chin, lip corners) using MediaPipe landmark geometry.
    *   Signature distance is verified frame-by-frame. Connection is killed and flagged if signature variation surpasses threshold (>0.20).
2.  **JPEG Compression Noise Defenses:**
    Enhances classifier resilience against high-frequency adversarial texture attacks:
    *   Implemented JPEG compression pre-processing directly into the face cropping stage.
    *   Strategically filters out artificial spatial noise injected by digital mock spoofs or adversarial camera filters.
3.  **Multi-Constraint Grid Search Weight Optimizer:**
    Calibrates decision fusion mathematically instead of using manual trial-and-error heuristics:
    *   Developed a rigorous weight tuning algorithm verifying 1,771 separate parameter models.
    *   Enforces a 10% minimum contribution check per sensor to eliminate sub-model silence.

## Slide 6: Interface Design & Live Demonstration

### Slide Content
*   **📱 Immersive Flutter Telemetry Panel:**
    *   **Real-Time Guidance Frame:** An interactive oval boundary changing colors based on quality metrics (Red: Pose mismatch, Blue: Poor lighting, Green: Perfect).
    *   **Explainable Verdicts:** Integrates real-time live telemetry cards showing dynamic confidence ratings for each modal sensor (texture, physiology, challenges).
    *   **Pre-verification Gateway:** Users are trained with tips and guidelines prior to starting high-stakes interview challenge verification sessions.
*   **🎥 Interactive Live Demonstration Protocol:**
    Our validation pipeline streams video frames from a Flutter client webcam to our FastAPI server over WebSockets:
    *   *Start verification:* A target challenge is selected randomly (e.g., blink task).
    *   *Intercept Attack:* We simulate printed photo attacks (rejected by rPPG and Texture sensors).
    *   *Intercept Replay:* We play digital replay loop (rejected by Active Challenge & Temporal validation).
    *   *Intercept Swap:* A second user swaps with candidate mid-session (rejected by scale-invariant landmarks check).
